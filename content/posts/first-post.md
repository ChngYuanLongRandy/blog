I've been meaning to start a blog for years. Between work, the kids, and the general chaos of life in Singapore, I never quite got around to it.

Then one evening, after putting the kids to bed and finally sitting down with a cup of tea that had gone cold, I thought: *just start*.

So here we are.

## What This Blog Is About

Honestly, I'm not entirely sure yet. I expect it'll be a mix of things:

- Parenting in Singapore — the good, the hard, and the absurd
- Random thoughts I can't get out of my head
- Things I've learnt, often the slow and painful way
- Maybe some food posts, because I'm Singaporean and it would be strange if there weren't

## Who This Is For

Mostly for myself. Writing helps me think. If something lands for you too, that's a bonus.

If you're another dad (or mum) somewhere in Singapore nodding along, hi. We're all figuring it out.

Let's see where this goes.
