Before I had kids, I thought I had a reasonable idea of what parenthood would look like. I was wrong about almost everything.

Here are a few things I wish someone had told me earlier.

## The Queue for Everything Starts Earlier Than You Think

Primary school registration. Enrichment classes. Weekend slots at the playground that isn't sweltering. If you're not planning six months ahead, you're already behind.

I don't say this to stress you out. I say it because once you accept it, you stop being surprised and start being strategic.

## "Kiasu" Is Just Another Word for Love

We give kiasu parents a hard time. But most of us, if we're honest, are doing the same thing — just in different domains. Some parents queue for elite schools. Some queue for character-building nature camps. The anxiety is the same. The love underneath it is the same too.

## The HDB Corridor Is Underrated

Our kids have made more friends in the corridor and the void deck than at any organised playdate. There's something irreplaceable about unstructured neighbourhood life. Don't optimise it away entirely.

## You Will Become Your Parents

I swore I'd never say certain things. I have said all of them. Twice.

---

This list is incomplete and always will be. Every kid is different. Every family finds its own way.

But if you're in the thick of it right now, know that it gets easier in some ways and harder in others, and that muddling through is not failure — it's the whole game.
