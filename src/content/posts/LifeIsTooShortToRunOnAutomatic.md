> Twenty years from now you will be more disappointed by the things you didn't do than by the things you did.
> — Mark Twain

It is important to realise that we are here for a limited number of time and sometimes it is easy to forget that work is not the sole reason for our existence.

How many of us have put off items after items in our bucket list or new year resolutions, telling ourselves that we have another year to do them?

Maybe it is to

- Try an open-mic in a comedy show
- Write a book
- Live in another country for a year
- Speak another language
- Develop a mobile app

Often times we think we are not good enough and do not even attempt to start them. More often than not its really the hustle and bustle of everyday life and then time passes by so fast. Before you know it, we look back and think to ourselves : what was I doing all this time?

Dig out your old childhood dreams or reach out for a pen and paper and jot down what are the things you have always wanted to do and start working on it because life is too short to waste.

Let's start cracking!
